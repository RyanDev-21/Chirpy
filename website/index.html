What it does

Your own pigeon
Pick the body, crest, eyes, hat and colours. It's your picture everywhere in Chirpy.
Pigeon messages and reactions
Send your pigeon waving, dancing, laughing or crying. React with your pigeon, not just an emoji.
Stories and notes
Share a photo with your friends for 24 hours, or leave a short note above your picture. Both disappear by themselves.
Streaks, if you like them
Chat with a friend every day to keep a streak going. Not your thing? Turn streaks off.
Private chats and groups
One-to-one, or groups with an admin and moderators. Private with an invite code, or public.
Voice calls
Free calls between friends that connect the moment you pick up. The audio goes straight between your phones, encrypted.
Voice notes, photos, files and GIFs
Videos up to 10 minutes, documents, and GIFs straight from your keyboard. Apps and programs are blocked.
Search in any language
Thai, Burmese, English, Chinese, even languages without spaces between words.
Every device, even offline
Up to five devices at once. Messages you send without a signal go out once you're back, never twice.
Your choices
Hide your last seen, mute chats or someone's stories, block and report, delete your account any time.

Install

Chirpy isn't on Google Play yet, so Android asks you to confirm the install. That's normal for apps from a website.

  1. Download. Tap Download for Android and open the file.
  2. Allow this source. If Android blocks unknown apps, tap Settings and turn on Allow from this source for your browser.
  3. Install. If Play Protect doesn't recognise the app, tap More details → Install anyway.
  4. Sign up with your email or with Google, then make your pigeon.

Only download Chirpy from this page. Compare the SHA-256 checksum under the button to check the file wasn't changed.

Your data

No ads. Never sold. Never used to train AI. Our admins can't read your messages, except ones someone reports to them.
