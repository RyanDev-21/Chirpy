Install in a minute

Chirpy isn't on Google Play yet, so Android asks you to confirm the install. That's normal for apps from a website.

  1. 1
    Download

    Tap Download for Android above and open the file when it's done.

  2. 2
    Allow this source

    If Android says installing unknown apps isn't allowed, tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source for your browser, then go back.

  3. 3
    Install

    Tap Install. If Play Protect doesn't recognise the app, tap More details → Install anyway.

  4. 4
    Sign up

    Open Chirpy and sign up with your email or with Google.

Only download Chirpy from this page. The SHA-256 checksum shown under the button lets you check the file wasn't changed. Chirpy tells you when a new version is out.

Your chats are yours

No ads. Your data is never sold, and never used to train AI. Our admins can't read your messages, except ones someone reports to them. Photos, videos and files are deleted from our servers within days.
