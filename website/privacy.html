We don't show ads, don't sell or share your data for advertising, and don't use your messages to train AI.

Who can see what

  • Your messages and media are seen by the people in that chat or group.
  • In groups of up to 50 people, the sender of a message can see which members have read it.
  • Your username, display name, bio and picture can be seen by other users. Your city and interests are shown only to friends, or to everyone if you turn on discovery.
  • Our admins can see account information (such as username, email address and activity counts) to run and protect the service. They cannot see your messages or media, except messages another user chose to attach to a report. Every admin action is logged.
  • Messages are encrypted while they travel to and from our servers and are stored with our providers' disk encryption, but Chirpy is not end-to-end encrypted: our servers can technically read messages in order to deliver and store them.

How long we keep it

  • Photos and videos: deleted from our storage 24 hours after everyone in the chat has received them, and after 7 days at most. Copies stay on the phones that downloaded them.
  • Messages: until you delete them (delete for everyone) or the account is deleted.
  • Sign-in sessions: up to 30 days of inactivity.
  • Email codes: 2 minutes.
  • Server logs: a short time, as kept by our hosting provider.
  • Reports: until handled, then 180 days.
  • Call history: 180 days.

Deleting your account

Delete your account in the app under your profile settings. This removes your profile, picture, friends, push tokens and sessions immediately, and your photos and videos within the next cleanup (about an hour). Messages you sent stay in the other person's or the group's history, shown as coming from a deleted account. You can also email us to ask for deletion.

Your rights

You can see and correct your profile in the app, delete messages and your account, and turn off discovery and individual notification types. Depending on where you live (for example under Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act or the EU's GDPR) you may also have the right to get a copy of your data, object to processing, or complain to a data protection authority. To use these rights, email privacy@ponypigeon.com.

Children

Chirpy is not for children under 13 (or the minimum age for this kind of service where you live). If you believe a child has an account, tell us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
